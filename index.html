Features

Built for the counter, not for an office

A new case while the client is still at the counter

Four types cover what shops deal with every day: special orders, repairs, sales leads and complaints. Rename them with your shop’s own words, or turn off what you don’t use.

  • 12 ready-made templates per type fill in urgency, details and the expected date
  • Follow-ups planned automatically: a repair after 5, 10 and 15 days, a lead after 2, 7 and 30
  • Photos, voice notes and dictation for the details

Reminders that don’t slip

When it’s time to call, the alert shows up on screen: mark it done, snooze it, or open the case. With the app closed, it arrives as a notification.

  • “Needs attention” comes first, with the reason: ready for pickup, overdue, follow-up due
  • List and case side by side on the iPad and the Mac

Close the loop with one swipe

Swipe right to resolve, left to delete. A lead closes as won, with the sale value, or lost, with the reason. Everything can be undone.

  • “Product arrived” and “Picked up” buttons for special orders
  • Repair stages with your own names, from “sent” to “delivered”

Your shop in numbers

See what happened this week, this month or ever, compared with the period before. Per store, or just your own cases.

  • Lead conversion and sales from converted leads
  • Repair time, measured until the item is ready
  • Where clients come from and why leads are lost

Your language, and your client’s

Each person uses the app in English, Italian, Portuguese or Spanish. Messages go out in the client’s language, worked out from the phone number or chosen on the case.

  • Every text written by hand, not machine-translated
  • Formal or informal: tu or Lei, tú or usted
  • Your company’s own message texts, signed by whoever sends them

Teams

One company, several stores, one team

Everyone in a store works on the same cases. Each case has a person responsible, who gets its reminders. When someone leaves, their open cases are handed to a colleague.

Questions shops ask

Does it replace my till or invoicing software?

No. Caderninho sits next to them and takes care of what they don’t: the customers who are waiting, the follow-ups and the messages.

Do my clients need to install anything?

No. Messages open in your own WhatsApp, already written, and you send them as usual.

Which devices does it run on?

iPad, Mac, iPhone, Android and any computer with a recent browser. Install it on the home screen and it works like an app.

Can I use my shop’s own words?

Yes. Rename the case types and the repair stages, turn off what you don’t use, and write the company’s own message texts.

Which languages?

The app speaks English, Italian, Portuguese and Spanish. Each person picks theirs, and messages go out in the client’s.

Is our data safe?

Each company’s data is separated by the database itself, sign-in is by invitation and two-step login is available. The app stores no addresses and no payment details.
